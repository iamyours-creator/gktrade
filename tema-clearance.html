Tema Port Customs Clearance: A Step-by-Step Guide

Published on: May 2026 | Category: Logistics

Your vehicle has successfully sailed from South Korea and has arrived at the Port of Tema, Ghana's busiest commercial hub. What happens next? Navigating the customs clearance process can be intimidating for first-time importers, but understanding the workflow ensures your vehicle is released quickly without incurring exorbitant demurrage (storage) fees.

Step 1: Obtain the Customs Classification and Valuation Report (CCVR)

Before your car arrives, you or your clearing agent must apply for a CCVR through the Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS). You will need the Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, and Packing List provided by GK Trade. The CCVR will officially determine the value of your car and calculate the exact duties, taxes, and potential over-age penalties you owe to the state.

Step 2: Payment of Duties and Taxes

Once the CCVR is issued, proceed to pay the assessed duties at any designated participating bank or through the official online portals. It is crucial to pay these fees promptly. The Port of Tema grants a limited number of 'free days' for storage. Once these days expire, you will be charged daily demurrage fees by the shipping line and rent by the Ghana Ports and Harbours Authority (GPHA), which can eat heavily into your profits.

Step 3: Physical Examination and Release

After payment is confirmed, Customs officials will conduct a physical examination of the vehicle. They verify the chassis number (VIN), engine number, and the overall condition of the car to ensure it matches the documentation. If you shipped personal items or spare parts inside the car, they will be inspected here.

Upon a successful inspection, Customs will issue a release order. You will then settle any terminal handling charges with the shipping line and GPHA. Finally, a gate pass is generated, allowing you to drive your newly imported Korean vehicle out of Tema Port and straight to the DVLA for registration.
