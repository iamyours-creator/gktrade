Why Import Used Cars from South Korea to Ghana?

Importing vehicles from South Korea to Ghana via Tema port has become increasingly popular among Ghanaian auto dealers and individual buyers. GK Trade is at the forefront of facilitating this seamless trade. Korean vehicles, particularly brands like Hyundai and Kia, offer an exceptional balance of affordability, durability, and fuel efficiency, making them perfectly suited for Ghanaian roads.

Unlike other markets, South Korea's well-maintained road infrastructure and strict vehicle inspection regulations ensure that used cars remain in excellent mechanical condition. Furthermore, the availability of spare parts for Korean LHD (Left-Hand Drive) vehicles in Accra and Kumasi is abundant, lowering the long-term maintenance cost for vehicle owners.

At GK Trade, we handle the entire process. From sourcing the best vehicles at Korean auto auctions to managing inland logistics, Ro-Ro shipping, and document preparation (Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice). We ensure compliance with the Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) customs regulations to help you avoid unnecessary over-age penalties.
