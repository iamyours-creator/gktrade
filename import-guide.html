The Comprehensive Guide to Importing Used Cars from South Korea to Ghana

Published on: May 2026 | Category: Import Guide

Importing a used vehicle from South Korea to Ghana has become one of the most reliable and cost-effective ways to acquire high-quality cars. With the increasing demand for durable, fuel-efficient vehicles like the Hyundai Elantra, Hyundai Tucson, and Kia Sportage, Korean exports to the Port of Tema are at an all-time high. This guide will walk you through why Korean cars are the best choice and how the import process works.

Why Choose South Korean Used Cars?

Unlike cars from other global markets, South Korean vehicles offer unique advantages for Ghanaian drivers. First, South Korea drives on the right side of the road, meaning all vehicles are manufactured as Left-Hand Drive (LHD). Ghana's traffic laws strictly prohibit the importation of Right-Hand Drive vehicles, making Korean cars ready for Ghanaian roads without any expensive steering conversion modifications.

Secondly, South Korea possesses world-class road infrastructure and enforces strict vehicle inspection laws. This means that a 5-year-old car from Korea typically has significantly less suspension wear and undercarriage damage compared to a car of the same age from countries with rougher terrains. Furthermore, the massive presence of Hyundai and Kia in Ghana ensures that spare parts are cheap and readily available in markets like Abossey Okai in Accra and Suame Magazine in Kumasi.

The Shipping Process: Ro-Ro vs. Container

When you purchase a car through GK Trade, we handle the logistics from the Korean dealer's lot directly to the Port of Incheon, and finally to the Port of Tema. There are two primary methods of shipping:

  • Ro-Ro (Roll-on/Roll-off): The vehicle is driven directly onto a massive vessel. It is generally the most cost-effective method for shipping single vehicles.
  • Container Shipping: The vehicle is loaded inside a 20ft or 40ft steel container. This method offers maximum security and allows buyers to ship spare parts or other goods alongside the vehicle.

Required Documentation

To successfully clear your vehicle at Tema, you will need a specific set of documents that GK Trade provides via international courier (DHL/FedEx) once the ship departs. These include the Bill of Lading (B/L), which serves as the receipt of cargo and contract of carriage; the Commercial Invoice, indicating the purchase price; and the Korean Export Certificate (deregistration document) translated into English.

By partnering with a transparent B2B supplier like GK Trade, you avoid hidden fees and ensure that all documentation is 100% accurate, preventing costly delays at the Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) customs checkpoints.
